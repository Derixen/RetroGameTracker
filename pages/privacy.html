Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RetroTrack Games collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account with RetroTrack Games, we collect the information necessary to provide and maintain your account.

  • Email address used for registration and authentication.
  • Account identifier and profile information.
  • Games added to your collection, including favorites and completion status.
  • Technical authentication and security information needed to operate the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

RetroTrack Games processes account and collection information to provide and maintain its services.

  • To create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • To save and display your personal game collection.
  • To remember your favorites and game completion status.
  • To provide account management and deletion functionality.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the platform.

3. Data Storage and Security

RetroTrack Games uses Supabase to provide authentication and database services. Account information and personal game collections are stored using this infrastructure.

Access controls are implemented to restrict access to personal collection information. Authentication and database security measures are used to help protect user data against unauthorized access.

4. Account Deletion and Your Rights

You can request deletion of your RetroTrack Games account through the account management features provided on the website.

Depending on applicable data protection laws, including the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of your personal information. You may also have the right to object to certain processing activities.

To exercise your privacy rights or ask questions about your personal information, please contact us through our Contact & Support page.

5. Third-Party Services

RetroTrack Games uses third-party services to operate and maintain the platform.

  • Supabase: Provides authentication, database storage, and backend functionality.
  • GitHub Pages: Hosts the website and delivers its frontend files.
  • IGDB: Provides video game metadata used to populate the game database.

6. Data Retention

RetroTrack Games generally retains account information and personal game collection data for as long as the account remains active.

When an account is deleted, associated personal data is removed according to the platform's deletion procedures, subject to applicable legal obligations and technical retention requirements.

Certain information may remain temporarily in backups or security logs before being deleted according to the relevant retention schedules.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, RetroTrack Games processes personal information under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual necessity: Processing necessary to provide user accounts, authentication, and personal game collections (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Legitimate interests: Processing necessary to maintain platform security, prevent abuse, and ensure reliable operation, where those interests are not overridden by users' rights (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Legal obligations: Processing required to comply with applicable laws (Article 6(1)(c)).

8. Data Controller and Contact

RetroTrack Games is an independently operated video game collection platform.

For privacy-related questions, requests concerning personal data, or complaints, please contact us through our Contact & Support page.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

RetroTrack Games uses browser storage technologies to support authentication and maintain user sessions. These technologies help keep users signed in and provide essential application functionality.

10. International Data Transfers

Some third-party service providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Where GDPR applies, such transfers must comply with applicable data protection requirements.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

RetroTrack Games uses browser storage technologies to support authentication, maintain user sessions, and provide essential application functionality.

These technologies may include cookies and local storage. The specific technologies used depend on the authentication and hosting services.

10. International Data Transfers

Some third-party service providers may process personal information outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Where applicable, international transfers must comply with GDPR requirements, including the use of appropriate transfer safeguards.

11. Children's Privacy

RetroTrack Games is a video game collection management platform. Its account registration and use requirements are described in the Terms of Service.

If you believe personal information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

12. Automated Decision-Making

RetroTrack Games does not currently intend to use personal information for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on users.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to RetroTrack Games, its functionality, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

The date displayed at the beginning of this policy indicates when it was last updated. Where required, users will be informed of material changes.
